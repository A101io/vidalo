## Cases to watch

- **Known change — Ferme Tred (11).** Google also lists *C470 Store* at the same address (Zwaanshals 470), marked "Tijdelijk gesloten", with an older profile. If ChatGPT names C470 Store or calls the shop closed, that is a wrong fact. This is the only change case I could prove from Google itself; I did not find four.
- **Split hours — Simba (2), Ta Lois (7).** Google holds two sets of hours (open and, probably, kitchen). Watch whether "tot hoe laat open" and "keuken open tot" get mixed up.
- **Short hours — SuperMatique (12).** Open two days a week. An answer that says open on a weekday is wrong.
- **No website — Jacob Cats (1), De Kluif (4), Bonkieskoek (17), Kapsalon Charlois (19).** Jacob Cats also has no phone listed: any phone number ChatGPT gives is unverified.
