Checked on 14 October 2026 against primary or authoritative sources on the web and against what the code actually does. Scope: `packages/core/src/copy/index.ts` (NL) and `en.ts` (EN), `docs/MESSAGING.md`, `docs/legal-compliance-guidelines.md`, `docs/CRITERIA.md`, `apps/web/components/AgentsSection.tsx`, the landing, pricing, how-it-works, why, straks, sector and FAQ copy, the legal drafts (`/privacy`, `/voorwaarden`), the footer identity line, `/llms.txt`, and the e-mail templates in `packages/core/src/reporter/templates.ts`. Code checked for the real intervals and behaviour: `packages/core/src/scanner/index.ts` (`SCAN_DEFAULTS`), `packages/core/src/scanner/engines/*`, `packages/core/src/fixer/paths.ts` (`FIX_PATHS`), `packages/core/src/publisher/channels/*`, `apps/worker/src/cron.ts`, `apps/web/app/api/checkout/route.ts`, `apps/web/app/r/[id]/page.tsx`.

Key paths below are relative to `copy.` in `index.ts`; the EN mirror has the same key path in `en.ts`.

## Update 5 Nov 2026 (branch `claude/copy-truth`)

Rechecked against `main` after `docs/reality-check-2026-10-25.md`. Changed in NL and EN, with the reason in the code:

- **Copilot** out of every customer string (landing engines row, sector check intro, scenes, before/after cards, sector examples, the "Waarom" description, own facts, assistant rule, support description). `scanner/engines/copilot.ts` is still a stub, so no scan asks it any more: `SCAN_DEFAULTS.free` is ChatGPT and Google AI (what the copy already said), the paid kinds use `ASKED_ENGINES` (all but Copilot). The demo report's third engine is Perplexity.
- **The eight sector "eigen steekproef" figures** (aannemers, VvE, zorg, garages, kappers, advocaten, makelaars, kinderopvang) are deleted; the sector page shows the two sourced figures. The installateurs "eigen inventarisatie, 13 gidsen" (a reading of the directory list in `publisher/channels/directories.ts`) and the two Insites figures stay.
- **Directories, Bing, Apple**: no automatic sync exists (`channels/bing.ts`, `apple.ts` and every directory end in an operator task; no directory has a `contactEmail`, so `fixer` sends no correction mail outside mock mode). "Dezelfde gegevens naar …", "zetten je in …", "dertien bedrijvengidsen" synchronisation, "Google-profiel direct via je login" and "dag 2: Google en gidsen gevuld" now say we ask for a correction or show what to change.
- **Google profile**: the adapter waits for Google's Basic API Access (go-live A3); the welcome mail says we update it once Google gives access.
- **"Wij herstellen de bron"** (hero caption, loket, step 4, pricing, FAQ, fix paths, mails) now says we find the source and ask for a correction, or show the owner what to change. Large platforms accept changes from the owner only.
- **Own facts** (`shared/own.ts`): the five connected AI systems, no directory sync, websites by request (no longer listed under "doen we niet").

## Summary

Of the roughly 60 checkable claims, the protocol facts in the "Straks" section (OKF, llms.txt, MCP, A2A, Agentic Commerce Protocol) are all correct, as are the Dutch legal references (21% btw, one-month AVG response, complaint at the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, EU-US Data Privacy Framework still in force), the VAT identity of A101 B.V., the fix-path intervals (7/14/30 days, 3 weeks for "not found"), the 90-day re-scan and the 7/21-day follow-ups.

Eight claims are wrong and should be edited before launch:

1. The three headline statistics on the landing page are mis-sourced or wrong: "45%, CBS 2025" is Eurostat 2025 (44.7%, 16–74); "37% verwacht dat het antwoord klopt, KPMG 2026" is wrong (KPMG/Melbourne 2025: 33% trust AI, 37% is the share that experienced negative outcomes); "1 op 5 begint een aankoop met een vraag, SearchLab 2026" could not be found anywhere.
2. The "61% / 22% / 17%" source split and the "Profound, 680 miljoen citaties" footnote do not exist in any Profound publication; the 680M figure belongs to a 5WPR/Everything-PR index and says nothing about directories vs own website.
3. The Ohio bookshop story is attributed to "Hoodline, juli 2026"; the real source is News 5 Cleveland (The Viridian Bookshop, Willoughby, 22 July 2026), the owner reported "weeks" of falling sales (not "a month with almost no revenue"), and the cause was not an old directory listing (unknown; the AI summary also gave a wrong address).
4. "Twintig keer" per question (controle scene, how-it-works meta description) versus 10 in `SCAN_DEFAULTS.monthly` (3 in the free check).
5. The free check and the plans promise Copilot, but the Copilot engine is a stub (`available()` is false unless `COPILOT_ENABLED=1` and a browser adapter is registered; none is). Today a free check asks ChatGPT and Google AI only.
6. Insites' 10,000-business study (US, ChatGPT and Perplexity only) is presented as "landelijk" and extended to "winkels … verkeerd adres" and "'gesloten' is de meest voorkomende fout bij horeca", neither of which the study measured.
7. "Deze rapportlink werkt 30 dagen" / privacy "30 dagen via de link te zien": no expiry is enforced in `apps/web/app/r/[id]/page.tsx`.
8. The footer identity line lacks the btw-id and a real person's name that `docs/MESSAGING.md` requires; VIES confirms NL869514192B01 = A101 B.V., Factorij 80, 3071 AV Rotterdam.

Five claims are outdated or inconsistent with the code (14-day third-party fix, "Google, Bing en Apple gelijkgezet binnen 48 uur", monthly engines three vs five, the unsubscribe sentence in the privacy text, llms.txt adoption wording). A further group (own-sample sector statistics, the Haarlem "47 van de 100" hero, the "60% of sources change per month" figure, KvK 42057858) could not be verified.

Style: no exclamation marks, consistent "je"; the only jargon leaks are "AI-agents" in the footer link and page title outside the Straks section, and "schema.org/llms.txt" in the own-facts meta description. The EN copy is faithful; a dozen phrases read as translated Dutch and are listed with fixes.
