# BE-08 — Mails, CLI markdown and e2e:mock for an online business

*lane: backend · estimate: 2 h · depends on: BE-03, BE-04, BE-06*

## Goal
Everything outside the report page that describes a check says the right thing for an online
business, and the mock pipeline proves the online path end to end. Serves AC-2 (CLI path), AC-7
(mails), AC-A4.

## Contract
- `scripts/e2e-mock.mts`: add a step "online business": `prepareBusiness({name:"Sonable", place:"Rotterdam"})`
  then a free scan; assert `report.profile.mode === "online"`, 6 questions, none containing
  "Rotterdam" or an hours/pinnen/address/phone word, `recommendations` with `rec_category` and
  `alternative` families, and a stip that is not grey for lack of checks.
- `packages/core/src/cli/scan.ts` markdown (`out/scans/*.md`): a "Gecontroleerd als" line (mode,
  offer, category) and, for online, the question list as asked; no town in the heading for online.
  `pnpm scan --name Sonable --place Rotterdam --kind free` runs the understanding step like any scan.
- Mails (`packages/core/src/reporter`, `pnpm mail:lab`): add an online business to the lab. For it,
  no line names the town as the business's place ("<naam>, Rotterdam" headings, "in Rotterdam"
  question wording from `questionFor`-style helpers) and no local wording ("openingstijden",
  "vestiging") appears. Lines that list recommendation questions keep their current block; the
  competition block is not added to mails (out of scope).
- `docs/mail-lab.md`: one line that the lab includes an online business.

## Done when
- [ ] `pnpm e2e:mock` green with the new step; `pnpm mail:lab` green and its checks cover the online business
- [ ] `out/scans/*.md` for the CLI Sonable run shows the profile line and town-free questions
- [ ] Tests written and shown to fail without the change
- [ ] No secret, no personal data in logs

## Not in this ticket
- Competition in mails, on `/app` or `/app/voortgang` (brief: out of this cycle).
- The real Sonable check on vidalo.ai (Julian, after deploy; QA reads it).
