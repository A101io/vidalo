# FE-01 — Company details on every page and on /contact

*lane: frontend (apps/web) · estimate: 0.25 d · depends on: BE-01 (keys only) · complex: no · contract: `../02-architecture.md` §3, §5, §6*

## Goal
The footer of every page and of the coming-soon page shows name, address, KvK, phone and btw
number; `/contact` names them and says how to complain. Serves AC-1, AC-2, AC-3.

## Acceptance criteria (from `01-brief.md`)
- **AC-1** — Given any page with `SiteFooter` or `ComingSoonFooter`, when the footer renders in NL and EN, then the company line contains name, address, KvK number, phone and the btw number NL869514192B01 (`footer.legal` in both files; the existing three-field line extended).
- **AC-2** — Given `/contact` and `/en/contact`, when the page renders, then it shows email, phone, hours (already there), and the company line with btw number, so that "Onze bedrijfsgegevens staan onderaan" is true.
- **AC-3** — Given `/binnenkort` and `/en/coming-soon`, when the page renders, then email and phone are visible and the footer carries the same company line (AC-1).

## Contract
Consumed: `copy.landing.footer.legal` (already rendered by `SiteFooter.tsx:22`, `:71` and
`ComingSoonFooter.tsx:12`; no code change needed there unless wrapping fails at 375 px),
`copy.pages.contact.company` (rendered at `app/contact/page.tsx:73`, unchanged code),
`copy.pages.contact.complaint` `{ h, p }` (new): render it on `/contact` as an `h2.h3` and a
paragraph between the "Handig om mee te sturen" block and the `never` line, design G
(Plus Jakarta Sans, ink on white, no new colour, no button; the page keeps WhatsApp as its one
primary button, `contact/page.tsx:19`). The addresses and phone inside `p` are text, not new
links (the mail and phone cards above already link them).

`e2e/company-details.spec.ts` (new):
- normal mode: `/`, `/en`, `/prijzen`, `/en/pricing`, `/contact`, `/en/contact` — footer text contains `A101 B.V.`, `Factorij 80`, `KvK 42057858`, `NL869514192B01`, `+31 85 109 1393`; `/contact` and `/en/contact` contain the complaint heading and `5 werkdagen` / `5 working days`;
- gate mode (`E2E_MODE=gate`): `/binnenkort`, `/en/coming-soon` — the same footer fields, and `hello@vidalo.io` and `+31 85 109 1393` visible above the footer;
- at a 375 px viewport on `/` and `/binnenkort`: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= 375`.
