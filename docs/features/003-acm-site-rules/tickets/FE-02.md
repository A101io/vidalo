# FE-02 — The Monthly card shows the total, the form says what follows, nothing is pre-ticked

*lane: frontend (apps/web) · estimate: 0.5 d · depends on: BE-01 (keys only) · complex: no · contract: `../02-architecture.md` §3, §5, §7*

## Goal
A visitor sees the full price of Vidalo Monthly in the price itself, reads under every email
field that two more mails follow and how to stop them, and finds no box ticked for them.
Serves AC-7, AC-9, AC-11.

## Acceptance criteria (from `01-brief.md`, AC-7 as amended by F-003)
- **AC-7** — Given the scan form (`ScanField`) and the report-email field (`EmailCapture`) in NL and EN, when rendered, then a sentence directly under the field says that two more messages follow and how to stop them, and links to the privacy statement. Wording: `copy.scanField.reminders` (§5; it says what the messages are about, F-003).
- **AC-9** — Given every `<input type="checkbox">` in `apps/web`, when searched, then none carries `checked` or `defaultChecked` except boxes that render a stored choice (§7 allow-list: controlled hours rows, stored mail preferences); a unit test pins that (control: adding `defaultChecked` to the marketing box fails it).
- **AC-11** — Given the Vidalo Monthly plan card in NL and EN, when rendered, then the price line reads "€149 eenmalig + €49 per maand, excl. btw" / "€149 once + €49 per month, excl. VAT" next to or instead of the bare "€49"; the sentence under the heading "Geen verborgen kosten" stays true.

## Contract
- `PriceBlock.tsx:56-57`: under the big `€49 per maand`, render `P.monitor.total` at 15 px bold, `text-white` on the blue card (not the 13 px `text-blue-light` of the limit line, which is the small print the brief objects to). `P.monitor.chip` (`:53`) renders the new chip unchanged in style. `P.monitor.limit` (`:67`) is shorter by itself.
- `ScanField.tsx`: after the email input and its error (`:67-85`), before the marketing box (`:86-89`), a `<p id="{id}-email-note" className="text-[13px] leading-[1.45] text-ink-2">` with `reminders.text` and a `Link` to `href("/privacy")` reading `reminders.link`; the email input gets `aria-describedby` that includes `{id}-email-note` (and still the error id when invalid). Always rendered, independent of the `note` prop (`:94`).
- `EmailCapture.tsx`: the same sentence under the field row (`:31-36`), above the error lines, with `aria-describedby="report-email-note"`. Not shown in the `sent` state.
- `packages/core/test/no-preticked-boxes.test.ts` (new; vitest in core reads `apps/web/**/*.tsx` as text, like `legal.test.ts` reads the source tree): finds every `type="checkbox"` element; fails when it carries `checked` or `defaultChecked`, unless it is in the §7 allow-list and its expression is not a literal `true`; always fails for `name="marketing"` with either attribute. It also asserts it found at least 3 checkboxes (control: the scan is not empty).
- `e2e/forms.spec.ts` (extend): on `/` and `/en` the sentence is visible under the email field and its link resolves to `/privacy` / `/en/privacy`; on a report page (`/r/voorbeeld` in mock) the same under the report field; on `/prijzen` and `/en/pricing` the Monthly card contains the total.
