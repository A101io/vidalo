# B-04 — The owner area is accessible (axe and keyboard over both lanes' pages)

*lane: B (Sonnet) · estimate: 4 h · depends on: B-01, B-02 (same lane)*

## Goal
Every page of AC-8 passes axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) with no serious or critical violation in NL
and EN in mock mode, and is operable by keyboard with visible focus. Serves AC-8.

## Ownership decision
Each lane owns the **markup** of its own pages and builds to the AC-8 rules in
`02-architecture.md` "Accessibility and language". This ticket owns the **test** over all of
them and fixes only lane B's pages (layout with the switcher, `/app/rapporten`, `/r/[id]`
expiry page). Lane A's pages (`/app/account` with sign-ins and "Overal uitloggen",
`/app/account/verwijderen`, `/app/abonnement` and its cancel and pause pages, `/app/google`
with disconnect, `/inloggen` with `opnieuw`, `/auth/confirm`) are in the spec by URL; before
merge their tests may fail on missing features and are marked `test.fixme` with the A ticket
named; after both lanes merge the delivery-lead removes the `fixme` marks and runs the whole
spec, and a violation on an A page is a finding routed to lane A.

## Contract
- `e2e/owner-a11y.spec.ts`: signs in as the mock demo owner (helper in
  `e2e/helpers-owner-area.ts`), reduced motion, cookie notice dismissed, then axe with
  `["wcag2a","wcag2aa","wcag21a","wcag21aa","wcag22aa"]` on, NL and `/en`:
  `/app` (with the switcher), `/app/rapporten`, `/app/voortgang`, `/app/account`,
  `/app/account/verwijderen`, `/app/abonnement`, `/app/abonnement/opzeggen`,
  `/app/abonnement/pauzeren`, `/app/google`, `/inloggen?opnieuw=1&next=/app`,
  `/auth/confirm?token_hash=x&type=email&next=/app`. Assert: no `serious`/`critical`.
- Keyboard: Tab from the top of `/app` reaches the switcher select and its button, every nav
  link and "Uitloggen", each with a visible focus outline (computed style), in order; choosing
  a business by keyboard alone switches it; the status line after switching is announced
  (`role="status"` contains the sentence).
- The dot is never the only carrier: on `/app/rapporten` every dot is `aria-hidden` and its
  row carries the meaning in text.

Consumes from lane A: those routes rendering in mock mode (`02-architecture.md` §5, "Every
page above renders in mock mode").
