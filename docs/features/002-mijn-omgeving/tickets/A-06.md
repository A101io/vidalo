# A-06 — Retention runs by itself; churn removes Google at once; the end state is explained

*lane: A (Opus) · estimate: 7 h · depends on: A-05 (same lane) · amended 11 Nov 2026 (F-010, F-012, F-015)*

## Goal
After a subscription ends: the Google token goes at once, `/app/abonnement` says what stopped
and when data goes, a reminder mail goes 14 days before deletion, and deletion happens on the
date, never for a running, paused or un-cancelled subscription; one recorded Stripe sandbox
run shows cancel, undo, cancel, pause, resume in the app and in Stripe. Serves AC-6.

## Contract
From `02-architecture.md` "Cron" and "End state":
- `packages/core/src/shared/db.ts` `setBusinessStatus`: when the status becomes `churned`,
  enqueue `google_disconnect { businessId, reason: "churn" }`.
- `apps/worker/src/cron.ts`: `export async function retention(now = new Date()): Promise<{ reminded: number; purgeQueued: number; googleQueued: number; overdue: number; dryRun: boolean }>;`
  steps 1–5 of the architecture incl. 3 (Scan Google end after `SCAN_GOOGLE_DAYS`, F-015)
  and 3a (`scanRetention` from `last_active_at` and `SCAN_RETENTION_MONTHS`, F-012; nothing
  when the constant is `null`); added to `cronTick` after `ownerSubscription`;
  `retention.overdue` logged once per Amsterdam day. `lifecycleTransitions` unchanged.
- `email` job template `deletion_reminder { businessId }` (`jobs/types.ts`, `jobs/misc.ts`),
  `sendDeletionReminder(b)` in `reporter/account-mails.ts`: the date, that reports can be
  downloaded until then, how to come back; service mail (not subject to mail prefs).
- `/app/abonnement` churned state: what stopped (checks, fixes, syncs, the facts page now
  sends visitors to the own website), reports readable until `deleteAt`, the date, Google
  removed. Copy in `copy.accountSecurity.endState`.
- `scripts/stripe-lifecycle-run.mts`: drives cancel → undo → cancel → pause → resume against
  the Stripe sandbox through the same `core/billing/lifecycle.ts` functions and prints each
  Stripe object state and event id for `05-verification.md` (run from Julian's Mac with
  sandbox keys, D2; this container cannot reach api.stripe.com).

Consumes from lane B: nothing.

## Tests
`apps/worker/test/retention.test.ts`, fake clock, MemDb, three businesses: A churned (the
subject), B active with `cancelAt` in the future (control), C active, owner-paused (control),
D active whose cancel was undone (control):
- day 0 churn → `google_disconnect` queued; after it runs A has no gbp channel; B, C, D keep theirs
- day 90 → the existing free check is queued for A
- day 106 → exactly one `deletion_reminder` for A; a second tick sends none
- day 119 → nothing purged; day 120 → one `purge` job for A; with `VIDALO_PURGE` unset it logs
  `retention.would_purge` and A remains; with `VIDALO_PURGE=1` A is gone
- with `VIDALO_PURGE` unset, an owner-scheduled deletion on its date **is** purged (F-010)
- a Scan business E (`plan setup`, `trial`): `delete_at` = last activity + 24 months; a sign-in
  at month 20 moves it and re-arms the reminder; reminder 14 days before; purged on the date
  (armed); its Google token is revoked 60 days after the first full check when no fix is open
  and no change is held, and kept while one is (F-015, F-012)
- B, C, D never get a reminder or a purge job across the whole run; `overdue` is 0 throughout
- an owner deletion scheduled for B's `cancelAt`: after Stripe's `subscription.deleted`
  (status churned) B is purged on that date without a reminder
- `pruneAuthThrottle` deletes rows older than 24 h only
