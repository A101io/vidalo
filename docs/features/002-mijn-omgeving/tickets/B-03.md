# B-03 — Owner pages stay fast as history grows

*lane: B (Sonnet) · estimate: 6 h · depends on: B-02 (same lane)*

## Goal
`/app`, `/app/voortgang`, `/app/rapporten`, `/app/account` and `/app/abonnement` answer in
under 1 s at p95 over 20 loads on a local production build and make the same number of
database round trips with 36 checks as with 3. Serves AC-7.

## Contract
From `02-architecture.md` "Scale", ADR-0007:
- `packages/core/test/helpers/counting-db.ts`:
  `export function countingDb<T extends Db>(db: T): { db: T; count(): number; reset(): void };`
  (Proxy: each Db method call counts 1; on PgDb each `sql` tagged-template call counts 1 and
  the method call that issued it is not counted twice).
- `apps/web/lib/core.ts` progress: `runSummaries(db, businessId, PROGRESS_MAX_SCANS)` instead
  of `buildSnapshot` per run; the snapshot is `summary.snapshot`. A run without a summary is
  left out of the counts (the backfill fills it).
- `/app` (`app/app/page.tsx`, `lib/owner-data.ts`, `lib/owner-log.ts`, `lib/reads.ts`): every
  list read gets an explicit limit (findings per status group ≤ 500, events for the log ≤ 200,
  runs ≤ 12) and no per-row query; each limit named in a comment with the page that shows it.
- Lane A's pages are measured, not edited: `/app/account` and `/app/abonnement` must meet the
  budget in `02-architecture.md`; a miss after merge is a finding routed to lane A.
- `scripts/perf/seed-history.mts`: on `verify:pg`'s database, one business with 36 monthly
  checks (10 questions × 5 AI systems × up to 10 answers, with claims), 500 findings, 2,000
  events, summaries written; and a second business with 3 checks.
- `scripts/perf/owner-pages.mts` (`pnpm perf:owner`): against a local production build
  (`scripts/local-prod.sh` or `next start` with `DATABASE_URL` = that database, mock sign-in
  as the seeded owner), 20 loads per page per business, prints p50/p95 and fails over 1 s.

Consumes from lane A: the routes `/app/account`, `/app/abonnement` by URL (exist today).
