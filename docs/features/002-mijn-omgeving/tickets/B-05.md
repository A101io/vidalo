# B-05 — Every changing action names its business

*lane: B (Sonnet) · estimate: 4 h · depends on: B-01 (same lane) · added 11 Nov 2026 (F-020)*

## Goal
With two businesses on one login and two tabs, a form rendered for business 1 can never
write to business 2 after a switch in the other tab: every changing action in lane B's files
acts on the business named in its form, re-checked for membership. Serves AC-2 ("act on
that business only"); security SEC-05.

## Contract
`02-architecture.md` §1a. Each action below reads `businessFromForm(form)` and passes
`requireOwnerOf` / `requireVerifiedOwnerOf` before any read or write; each form that renders
it carries `<input type="hidden" name="business" value={business.id}>`. No `business`, a
non-member or an unknown id → `redirect(href("/app"))`, nothing changed.
- `apps/web/app/app/gegevens/actions.ts`: `saveFacts`, `saveProfile` (verified),
  `reconfirmFacts` (owner); forms in `gegevens/page.tsx`, `FactsForm.tsx`, `ProfileGroup.tsx`
  and the layout's "Klopt dit nog?" form.
- `apps/web/app/app/actions.ts` (lane B's alone after C-00 moved `setLanguage` and
  `deleteMyData` out): `markFixed`, `approveChannelDiff`, `declineChannelDiff`, `checkNow`
  (verified), `dispute` (owner). `fouten/[id]/page.tsx` and `vermeldingen/[id]/page.tsx`
  resolve the business from the finding / channel and call `requireOwnerOf(its businessId)`
  instead of comparing with the active business. Lane A's account page also posts to
  `checkNow` and sends the hidden field (A-02).
- `apps/web/app/api/domain/verify/route.ts`: `business` from the form, `isMember` +
  `isVerifiedOwner`, and a unique violation on `business_facts_host_verified_uniq` handled as
  "taken" (`domain.refused`, `dns=nee`); `domein/page.tsx` sends the field.
- `apps/web/app/api/checkout/route.ts`: `onlyOwnerMayBuy` passes when the session's login
  `isMember` of that business (was: equals the active business).
- `apps/web/app/api/lang/route.ts` + `apps/web/components/LangSwitch.tsx`: the owner layout
  passes `business`; the route saves the language only for a business the login is a member
  of; otherwise only the redirect (as for visitors). `next` through `safeNext`.

Consumes from lane A: `safeNext` (C-00 body).
