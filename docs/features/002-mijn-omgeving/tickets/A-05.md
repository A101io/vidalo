# A-05 — "Verwijder alles" on every plan; one purge module guarded by the database

*lane: A (Opus) · estimate: 10 h · depends on: C-00; A-03 (`requireFreshSignIn`) and A-04 (`disconnectGoogle`) in the same lane · amended 11 Nov 2026 (F-010, F-012, F-013, F-014, F-019, F-021)*

## Goal
A Vidalo Scan, never-paid or churned owner deletes at once; a running Vidalo Monthly is
cancelled at period end and deleted on that date, revocable until then; a deletion removes
the business and its rows, unrun jobs, the custom facts host and its route, the Google token
(revoked), the login when no business remains, and sends a confirmation mail. Serves AC-5
(b), (c) for delete, (d).

## Tests
- `deletion.test.ts`: Scan (`plan setup`, `trial`) → now; never paid (`scanned`) → now;
  churned → now; Monthly active → at_period_end needsCancel; on PgDb a Scan business accepts a
  retention `delete_at` and a Monthly in `trial` refuses one (CHECK); Monthly cancelled → at = cancelAt;
  Monthly paused (owner pause, failed payment) → at_period_end.
- `db-account.test.ts` (MemDb and PgDb), each with a **control business that must survive**:
  `purgeBusiness(id, "no_subscription")` refuses a running Monthly and deletes a Scan owner;
  `due` refuses before the date and for status `active`; deletes after; returns false the
  second time; unrun jobs of that business gone, the control's kept; CHECK: setting
  `delete_at` on an active business without `cancel_at` fails on PgDb; trigger: status →
  churned sets `churnedAt` and `deleteAt = +120 d` (or keeps an owner's earlier date); clearing
  `cancelAt` on an active business clears the deletion; `setDeletion(null)` on a churned
  business falls back to `churnedAt + 120 d`.
- `purge.test.ts` with fake deps: order of calls; with `VIDALO_PURGE` unset an owner purge
  (inline `no_subscription`, fallback job, `due` with reason `owner`) deletes and a
  retention purge does not (F-010, one case per path); `stripeLive` true → `stripe_live`,
  nothing deleted, Stripe throwing → nothing deleted (F-021); a host held verified by another
  business, or stored unverified here, is not sent to Cloudflare (F-019); Google revoke failure continues;
  Cloudflare failure throws before the delete; own fallback host is not sent to Cloudflare;
  login deleted only when the member has no business left (two businesses, one login: first
  purge keeps the login, second deletes it); dry run deletes nothing; mail failure logged
  without the address; `account.deleted` carries no id.
- `e2e/account.spec.ts` (mock): the Scan owner deletes (with a fresh sign-in), lands on
  `/inloggen?verwijderd=1`, signing in again shows no business; the Monthly owner schedules,
  sees the refund and invoice sentences before the button, gets the `deletion_scheduled`
  mail at once (mock outbox), sees the date on `/app/account` and `/app/abonnement`, calls it off; a stale sign-in
  (signedInAt moved back) is sent to `/inloggen?opnieuw=1` and returns to `/app/account/verwijderen`;
  a hand-edited `business` of another login → `/app`, nothing changed. NL and EN.
