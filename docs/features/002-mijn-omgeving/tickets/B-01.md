# B-01 — Membership, the active business, and the switcher

*lane: B (Sonnet) · estimate: 8 h · depends on: C-00 · amended 11 Nov 2026 (F-017, F-019)*

## Goal
A login with more than one business sees each by name and place, switches between them, and
every owner page acts on the chosen one; a business of another login is never listed or
reachable by a changed value. Serves AC-2.

## Contract
From `02-architecture.md` shared contract §1, "Data model 0016", "Authorisation model",
ADR-0001:
- `supabase/migrations/0016_business_member.sql` exactly as specified (table, partial unique
  index, user index, RLS `member_self`, revokes, `sync_business_owner_member` trigger with
  `security definer set search_path = ''` and execute revoked, backfill, the seven 0001
  policies dropped and recreated as `member_*` with `(select auth.uid())`), plus Amendment 1:
  `revoke select on channel from anon, authenticated` and a column grant without `meta`

  first and record it).

- `packages/core/src/db/owner-area.ts`: `listMemberBusinesses`, `listMemberUserIds`,
  `isMember`, `linkBusinessesByEmail` — PgDb through `business_member`; MemDb derived from
  `db.businesses` (`ownerUserId`). Signatures unchanged from C-00.
- `apps/web/lib/active-business.ts`: `listMyBusinesses(session)` = `linkBusinessesByEmail`,
  owner address follows a changed login address (today's `account.email_changed` event, per

- `apps/web/components/BusinessSwitcher.tsx`: shown in `app/app/layout.tsx` only when

- Copy `copy.ownerArea.switcher` (`label`, `submit`, `current`), NL and EN.
- `apps/web/lib/demo-owner-area.ts`: the demo owner (`eigenaar@bakkerijdevries.nl`,

## Tests
- `db-membership.test.ts` (MemDb and PgDb): two businesses on login 1, one on login 2 →
  `listMemberBusinesses(1)` returns two, newest first, never login 2's; changing
  `ownerUserId` moves the member row (Pg: trigger); `isMember(1, b3) === false`;
  `linkBusinessesByEmail` links only unowned businesses with that address; deleting a
  business removes its member row (cascade; Mem: derived).
- `scripts/rls/membership.sql`: one `DO` block on `verify:pg`'s database (auth shim) that
  sets role `authenticated` and `request.jwt.claim.sub` for login 1, counts visible rows in
  `business`, `facts`, `finding`, `fix`, `channel`, `event`, `scan_run`, `business_member`,
  then for login 2 and anon, attempts an insert into `business_member`, and `raise exception`
  with the counts so it rolls back (CLAUDE.md pattern). Login 1 (control) must see rows;
  login 2 only its own; anon none; the insert refused; `select meta from channel` as login 1
  raises (permission denied) while `select id, status` returns rows (F-017); a second
  business marking the same host verified raises a unique violation (F-019). Run on the hosted project only by the
  delivery-lead after migrations are applied.
- `e2e/owner-area.spec.ts` (mock, NL and EN): one-business owner sees no switcher; the demo
  owner switches to the Amsterdam business, every nav page shows it, the choice survives a
  reload; posting `chooseBusiness` with the control business's id leaves the active business
  unchanged; `requireOwnerOf(<control id>)` called from a test action redirects to `/app`.
  (The hand-edited `business` on lane A's forms is asserted in A-05's spec.)
