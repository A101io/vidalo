# BE-06 — Monthly mail: one section per line; mail lab; e2e:mock with a line

*lane: backend · estimate: 3 h · depends on: BE-04, BE-05*

## Goal
The monthly report mail has a section per line in the owner's language, and the whole mock
pipeline includes a line. Serves AC5, AC7.

## Contract
`02-architecture.md`, "Mails" (`LineMailSection`, the wording rule).

Files:
- `packages/core/src/reporter/templates.ts`: `MonthlyReportData.lines?`, `LineMailSection`;
  `monthlyReportEmail` renders the sections after the business blocks and before the CTA; nothing
  when `lines` is empty or absent (a business without lines gets today's mail byte for byte).
- `packages/core/src/reporter/index.ts` `sendMonthlyReport`: builds `lines` from `listProductLines`,
  the month's line run per (line, market) (`listScanRuns(…, 1, { productLineId, market })`, its
  summary), and `listFindings(business.id, OPEN, { productLineId })`; `detailUrl` localized with
  `business.language`.
- `packages/core/src/copy/index.ts` + `en.ts`: `mails.monthly.lines` = `title(name)`,
  `market(marketName, stipWord, open, named)`, `notChecked(marketName)`, `more(n)`, `cta`. No price,
  no score, no position.
- `scripts/mail-lab.mts`: a monthly mail for the demo owner with both seed lines (NL and EN);
  checks: no score words, both languages.
- `scripts/e2e-mock.mts`: the pipeline business gets one line (two markets) after setup, runs the
  monthly check and the two line runs, the fix round, and the report; asserts the line runs, a line
  finding with a fix, and the line section in the written mail.

## Done when
- [ ] `pnpm mail:lab` shows the monthly mail with two line sections in NL and in EN; a market without a run reads "nog niet gecontroleerd" / "not checked yet" (AC5)
- [ ] Template test: a business without lines renders exactly today's mail (snapshot)
- [ ] `pnpm e2e:mock` green with the line (AC7); `VIDALO_DB=pg pnpm e2e:mock` green under `verify:pg`
- [ ] `pnpm -r typecheck && pnpm -r test` green; tests shown to fail without the change
- [ ] No secret, no personal data in logs
