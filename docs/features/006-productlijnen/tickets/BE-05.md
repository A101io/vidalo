# BE-05 — Worker and fixer: monthly line runs, gating, the report wait, line fix paths

*lane: backend · estimate: 4 h · depends on: BE-01, BE-04*

## Goal
Confirmed active lines enter every monthly check, only for businesses that may have them; line
findings get fixes and rechecks that are honest about what we can do. Serves AC2, AC4, AC5 (timing).

## Contract
`02-architecture.md`, "Worker" and ADR-0010, ADR-0012, ADR-0014.

Files:
- `apps/worker/src/jobs/types.ts`: `ScanJob.productLineId?`, `.market?`; `ReportJob.lines?`, `.waited?`.
- `apps/worker/src/cron.ts` `enqueueMonthlyScans`: the line jobs after the business job, as specified.
- `apps/worker/src/jobs/paid.ts`: line scans and line rechecks guarded as specified
  (`productLineAccess(business, business.ownerUserId)`, `lineScanRefusal`), refusals logged as
  `job.skipped {kind, reason}`.
- `apps/worker/src/jobs/scan.ts`: pass `productLineId`/`market`; a business monthly run's `report`
  step carries `lines`.
- `apps/worker/src/jobs/misc.ts` `report`: the bounded wait (`LINE_REPORT_WAIT_MINUTES = 15`,
  `LINE_REPORT_MAX_WAITS = 8`, both in that file), event `report.waiting {waited, missing}`.
- `apps/worker/src/jobs/spend.ts`: spread key includes `productLineId` and `market`.
- `packages/core/src/fixer/paths.ts`: `LINE_KEEP_CHECKING_PATH` (NL + EN labels, unfixable notes);
  `fixPathFor(sourceType, verdict, evidenceKind, productLineId?)` and `fixPathForFinding` per
  ADR-0014; `isFindabilityFinding` false for line findings.
- `packages/core/src/fixer/index.ts`: `runRecheck` uses `lineScopeOf(finding)` for `listPrompts` and
  passes `productLineId`/`market` to `runScan`; `RunScanOptions` there gains both.
- `packages/core/src/fixer/email-templates.ts`: owner instruction and correction request name the
  line ("over je productlijn {naam}") and word the four keys (from `facts-nl.ts`/`facts-en.ts`).

## Done when
- [ ] Cron test: a business with 2 active lines × markets (NL, BE) and 1 discontinued line gets 1 business + 4 line jobs, once per month; a Vidalo Scan business gets none
- [ ] Gate tests: line scan skipped for not paying, paused, not verified, `plan: "setup"`, discontinued line (monthly), a market no longer in the version; allowed for a recheck of a discontinued line
- [ ] Report test: waits while a line run is missing or running, at most 8 times, then sends; sends at once when all are done or failed
- [ ] Fixer tests: a line `third_party` finding queues a correction mail naming the line; `owned_website` mails the owner; `not_found` and `none` open `LINE_KEEP_CHECKING_PATH` and never call `publishFactsPage` or `syncAllChannels`
- [ ] Recheck test: `markFixedByOwner` on a line finding queues a recheck that runs a line run for that market and engine and resolves the finding when the answer is right (AC4)
- [ ] `pnpm -r typecheck && pnpm -r test && pnpm e2e:mock` green; tests shown to fail without the change
- [ ] No secret, no personal data in logs
