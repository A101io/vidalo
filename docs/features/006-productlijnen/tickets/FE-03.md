# FE-03 — Line detail: per market dots, open errors, "Wie AI wel noemt", trend

*lane: frontend · estimate: 5 h · depends on: BE-01*

## Goal
Per line and per market, after every monthly check: one dot per AI system, open errors with their
source, "genoemd als aanrader" n of m with who else was named, and the trend as on
`/app/voortgang`. Serves AC3.

## Contract
Consumed (BE-01): `Db.getProductLine`, `Db.listProductLineVersions`,
`runSummaries(db, businessId, 12, { productLineId, market })` → `ArchiveRow.summary` (`stip`,
`snapshot`, `recommend`), `Db.listFindings(businessId, undefined, { productLineId, market })`,
`openFixesByFinding`, progress functions (`engineGrid`, `criteriumGrid`, `changeOver`, `inPeriod`,
`snapshotStip`), `groupByQuestion` and `fixPlan` (`lib/owner-data.ts`), `copy.criteria[13..16]`.

Files:
- `apps/web/lib/product-lines.ts`: `loadProductLineDetail(id, business): Promise<LineDetail | null>`
  (null when `line.businessId !== business.id`): `line`, `versions: number`, and per market
  `{ market, snapshots: ScanSnapshot[] (oldest first), latest: ArchiveRow | null, recommend, open: FindingGroup[], resolved: Finding[] }`.
- `apps/web/app/app/productlijnen/[id]/page.tsx` (new): `requireOwner`; `notFound()` for another
  business's id. Header: name, category, status, "Versie n, bevestigd op <date>", "Bewerken"
  (`?bewerken=1`, FE-02's form) and the status button (FE-02). Market tabs as links
  (`?markt=<code>`, `aria-current`). For the chosen market:
  1. **Laatste controle**: date, one `Dot` per AI system with label and colour word.
  2. **Fouten**: open groups (question line from `copy.factKey` + subject, "n van m keer", source host, what we do from `fixPlan`), each linking to `/app/fouten/[id]`; resolved this period as proof lines.
  3. **Wie AI wel noemt**: "Genoemd als aanrader: n van m keer"; the other names with their counts; never a position or order claim.
  4. **Verloop**: the engine grid and criterium grid over the snapshots (same components and rules as `/app/voortgang`; extract the shared ones into `components/` only if both pages use them unchanged), and `changeOver` as counts.
  "Nog niet gecontroleerd" when the market has no run.
- `/en/app/product-lines/[id]` via FE-01's path map.
