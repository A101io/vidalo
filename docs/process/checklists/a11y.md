# Standing accessibility checklist

Owned by design-validator. **WCAG 2.2 AA is the standard, and it blocks** — not because a law
says so today, but because every brief carries an accessibility acceptance criterion, and a
failing acceptance criterion is a QA P0.

*On the law:* the European Accessibility Act exempts microenterprises providing services
(fewer than 10 staff, at most €2M turnover or balance sheet), which A101 B.V. very probably
is. So accessibility is **policy here, not legal obligation**, and compliance must not call it
a legal hard stop. Re-check the exemption when A101 passes either threshold. If a brief ever
lacks an accessibility AC, that is a finding against the brief.

## Every screen
- [ ] Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, ≥ 3:1 for large text and for the boundary of any control
- [ ] Every interactive element reachable and operable by keyboard alone, in a sane order
- [ ] Focus is always visible and never trapped
- [ ] Touch targets ≥ 44×44 px with real spacing between them
- [ ] Nothing is communicated by colour alone
- [ ] Every control has an accessible name that says what it does, not "button"
- [ ] Form errors are announced, associated with their field, and readable without colour
- [ ] Headings nest properly; landmarks present; skip link on long pages
- [ ] Text reflows at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling
- [ ] Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`
- [ ] Works in every theme the venture ships, and at the OS's large-text setting
- [ ] A fixed banner or bar never hides the focused element (SC 2.4.11); `scroll-padding`
      alone does not do it — pad the body by the same amount, and measure at phone width

## Copy quality gate (validator owns this too)
- [ ] Every language the venture ships reads as written by a person, not translated by one
- [ ] No jargon the venture's customer would not use
- [ ] Nothing promises something the product does not do
